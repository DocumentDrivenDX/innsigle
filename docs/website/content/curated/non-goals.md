# Non-goals

Category integrity matters as much as features. Innsigle is **content provenance
in the time of AI**. It is not:

- **An AI detector** — no scores, no SynthID decode, no authorship percentages.
- **A C2PA replacement** — media-pipeline Content Credentials stay in their lane.
- **A purity product** — human is not the only first-class state.
- **A guarantee of truth** — declaration plus optional signature only.
- **“Sloptimized = human”** — prose cleanup does not rewrite composition.
- **A multi-tenant social network** in v1.
- **Compliance certification** for the EU AI Act or similar.

**Sloptimizer** (easel-skills) cleans AI-shaped prose. Innsigle records how work
was made. Use both in sequence; do not launder authorship.
